      How to read this page

      Official numbers only is the result of the first dialer run on the numbers in the Ministry's PESA list (03/07/2026). A unit counts as having a working number when at least one of its numbers was answered by a person, or reached a menu, voicemail or an unclear connection. Everything else either failed (carrier message, network, blocked) or had no number in the list.

      With candidates adds every unit that has at least one candidate number from another source. It is the ceiling if every candidate works, not a result: the dialer starts calling them on 16/11, and a candidate only counts as found when a person answers and confirms the unit.

      Picking a source counts only that source's candidates, so the page shows how much each source alone would add. Some candidates are shared office lines (the town's or the state's health department), marked in the note.

      To update after the run: import the phone export, rebuild the call dashboard, run build_targets.py, build_candidates.py and build_page.py in reports/number-hunt-2026-11, and republish. Nothing here writes to app/hospitals.json.
