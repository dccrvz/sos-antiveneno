Validation loop

From candidate to published number

  1. All candidates never dialled before are loaded on the Samsung (installed 15/11) and go ahead of anything else. Retries of rejected numbers are off.
  2. The run starts 16/11 as day 1 of 17 and ends 02/12: Vivo, 99 a day, 4-minute spacing, 09–12h and 14–18h. 17 × 99 = 1,683 slots for the queue.
  3. Import the export ZIP with import_calls.py; the dashboard picks the new attempts up.
  4. A number counts as found only when a person answers and confirms the unit name. Carrier messages and voicemail do not count.
  5. score_sources.py rates each source and each repair trick by the share of numbers a person answered, against the first run's 11.6% and the retries' 1%.
  6. Confirmed numbers go into SoroJá as a traceable correction on top of the Ministry data, with the call as evidence. Dead official numbers are reported back to the state.
